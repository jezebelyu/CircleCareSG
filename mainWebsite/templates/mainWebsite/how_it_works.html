How It Works

Simple. Safe. Reliable.

CircleCareSG connects seniors, volunteers, guardians and administrators to provide a seamless care and support system.

How It Works - Step by Step

Register and Complete Profile

Create an account as one of the roles provided and provide some basic information so we can understand your needs.

Get Matched

We match seniors with suitable volunteers for community support.

Regular Welfare Checks

Volunteers support seniors through scheduled visits and welfare-related activities.

Receive Notifications or Alerts

Guardians and volunteers can review relevant updates and alerts through their portals.

Continuous Community Support

We are here to support, care and grow together.

Our Community Roles

Senior

  • Complete wellbeing check-ins
  • View assigned volunteers
  • Request support when needed

Volunteer

  • Manage availability
  • Review scheduled visits
  • Submit visit reports

Guardian

  • Monitor linked Senior information
  • Review alerts
  • Stay connected
