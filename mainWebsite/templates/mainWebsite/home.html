How We Support Seniors

Welfare Check-ins

Regular welfare visits and phone calls to ensure seniors remain safe, healthy and socially connected.

Volunteer Support

Building meaningful relationships through companionship and community engagement.

Meal Assistance

Helping seniors access meal support programmes and food assistance services.

Family & Guardian Support

Keeping loved ones informed through welfare updates and monitoring.

Creating a More Caring Singapore

CircleCareSG aims to reduce social isolation by bringing together volunteers, families and community organisations into one support network.

Through regular welfare check-ins and community engagement, seniors can remain connected, supported and cared for.
