About Us

Together, we build a caring community where no senior is left behind.

CircleCareSG is a community-based platform that connects seniors, volunteers, guardians and community partners to provide companionship, monitor wellbeing, and support seniors in living with dignity and independence.

Our Mission

To enhance the well-being of seniors through regular check-ins, community support and meaningful connections.

Our Vision

A society where every senior is cared for, connected and empowered to live independently and confidently.

Our Values

  • Compassion
  • Inclusivity
  • Respect
  • Integrity
  • Community

Our Story

As Singapore’s population ages, more seniors are living alone or with limited family support. Many may face challenges such as isolation, reduced mobility and difficulty accessing help.

CircleCareSG was created to bridge this gap by bringing together volunteers, families and community organisations into one platform. Through regular welfare check-ins and a strong support network, we aim to ensure seniors receive the care and companionship they need.

We believe every act of care creates a ripple of impact.

Who We Serve

Seniors

Elderly individuals who may need regular check-ins, companionship and community support.

Guardians & Families

Family members who want to stay informed and involved in their loved one's well-being.

Volunteers

Compassionate individuals who dedicate their time to support and uplift seniors.

Community Partners

Organisations and social service agencies working together to support seniors.
