Looking to become a Guardian?

Guardian accounts cannot be created through public registration. To protect our seniors' privacy and security, Guardian access is provided only after CircleCareSG has verified your relationship with the senior and your understanding of their situation.

Please contact our team with the relevant details. Once verified, an administrator will create your Guardian access and link you to the appropriate senior.
